ARTIST PHILOSOPHY & STATEMENT

The Living Archive of River & Rural Labour

“The river is not only a landscape; it is a living archive of labour, movement, memory and belonging.”

Mehedi Islam Rafi is a Bangladeshi contemporary visual artist and doctoral researcher at the Faculty of Fine Art, University of Dhaka. Working primarily in watercolour and acrylic, his practice is shaped by the rivers, boats, rural settlements, and working communities of Bangladesh.

Through fluid washes, atmospheric colour, and economical brushwork, he transforms observed moments into poetic visual narratives that honor everyday human dignity.
