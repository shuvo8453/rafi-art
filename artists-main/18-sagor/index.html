Artist Profile
Saiful Islam Sagor Visual Artist & Curator

Saiful Islam Sagor is a Bangladeshi visual artist and curator working across fine art, sculpture, and material-based studio practice, currently serving at the National Sculpture Gallery, Bangladesh.

His artistic activity encompasses solo and group exhibitions, selected international art expositions, artist workshops, and community-based engagements. Having participated in major platforms across Nepal, Sri Lanka, Indonesia, and Bangladesh, his studio practice is driven by deep tactile sensitivity and continuous experimentation with raw materials.

Alongside his personal creative oeuvre, his curatorial leadership spans key institutions including the National Sculpture Gallery, Samdani Art Foundation, Bengal Foundation, Gallery Cosmos, and Drik — fostering cross-cultural artistic exchange, exhibition production, and public dialogue in contemporary visual art.

◈ ARTIST STATEMENT ◈
Philosophy & Vision

Materiality, Form & Curatorial Dialogue

“My practice explores the tension between material substance, three-dimensional presence, and shared human encounter. Through sculpture, fine art, and tactile experimentation, I interrogate how raw materials hold cultural memory, and how curatorial frameworks activate dialogue between the creative object, architectural space, and the wider community.”
01 // TACTILITY

Material Exploration

Investigating the physical texture, density, and expressive possibilities of organic and raw studio media.

02 // SPATIALITY

Sculpture & Form

Shaping three-dimensional relationships between sculpted volumes, surrounding light, and the observer's gaze.

03 // CURATION

Curatorial Engagement

Structuring exhibition narratives that link artist studios, institutional galleries, and diverse public audiences.

04 // EXCHANGE

Cross-Cultural Exchange

Fostering South Asian and international dialogue through art expos across Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Indonesia.
