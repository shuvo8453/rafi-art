CURATED FINE ART COLLECTION

Selected Works (2019–2026)

A chronological exhibition of 30 curated works — featuring watercolour landscapes, terracotta and metal sculptures, site-specific installations, and archival documentation across Bangladesh.

2026

Silent Passage

Watercolour on paper

A study of working boats held in still water, where the quiet rhythm of river travel meets the openness of the landscape.

2026

Golden Riverbank

Watercolour on paper

Warm amber tones illuminate a river settlement, tracing the relationship between boats, palm trees, homes and daily labour.

2026

Life Along the River

Watercolour on paper

Across an expansive riverbank, travellers and vessels become small but vital marks in a landscape shaped by water.

2026

Reflections of Rural Life

Watercolour on paper

Palms, human figures and their reflections establish a delicate balance between habitation and the surrounding river.

2026

Island of Silence

Watercolour on paper

A flooded landscape becomes a meditation on fragility, adaptation and the persistence of community.

2026

Floating Homestead

Watercolour on paper

Vernacular dwellings and vegetation emerge from a broad expanse of water, suggesting endurance within seasonal change.

2025

Boats Beneath the Palms

Watercolour on paper

Traditional covered boats anchor a calm composition of palms, shorelines and layered reflections.

2025

Whispers of the Floodplain

Watercolour on paper

With economical brushwork and open paper, the image describes human passage through the quiet floodplain.

2025

Golden Shores

Watercolour on paper

An elongated panorama of trees, river craft and people captures the rhythms of settlement beside the water.

2025

The River’s Edge

Watercolour on paper

A narrow path leads toward a waterside dwelling, framed by a bare tree and an atmosphere of stillness.

2025

A Quiet Crossing

Watercolour on paper

Two palms and a handful of boats punctuate a wide river vista, giving small human gestures room to resonate.

2025

Memories of the Village

Watercolour on paper

The clustered forms of village architecture, riverbank and boats evoke a place remembered through atmosphere.

2024

Riverine Silence

Watercolour on paper · 15 × 22 inches

A serene watercolour narrative capturing the quiet morning calm on the river. Soft translucent washes and delicate light transitions reflect the floating wooden boat, distant tree-lined banks, and the vast open sky.

2024

Ferry Crossing & River Life

Watercolour on paper · 15 × 22 inches

An expansive watercolour composition depicting a country ferry carrying workers and bicycles across open waters. Surrounded by traditional fishing boats and billowing sky washes, it documents the vitality and riverine heritage of rural Bangladesh.

2024

Paddy Fields & Village Path

Watercolour on paper · 15 × 22 inches

A vivid landscape recording rural mobility along a narrow village path flanked by flooded paddy fields. Rich atmospheric washes of warm golden light and clouds harmonize with coconut palms and traditional homesteads at dusk.

2024

Expressions of Humanity

Terracotta & Ceramic Sculpture

An intricate terracotta figurative sculpture featuring layered, expressive human faces in dynamic contortion, exploring human vulnerability, psychological states, and collective consciousness.

2023

Earthen Circle

Site-Specific Installation & Performance

Overhead documentation of an environmental installation with women in traditional white and red sarees positioned within a circle of earthen clay pots, honoring folk rituals and ancestral soil connections.

2023

Relic Study — Clay & Steel

Terracotta & Weathered Steel Assemblage

A tactile sculptural study juxtaposing an expressive hand-moulded terracotta figurine head with an oxidized industrial steel bar, exploring the passage of time, material decay, and permanence.

2023

Marking the Silence

Live Performance & Body Art

Photographic documentation of an immersive live performance where the artist, adorned in white ceremonial pigment, inscribes spontaneous gestural marks across the wall in active dialogue with observers.

2023

Studio Residency & Sculptural Busts

Documentary Studio Archive

Mehedi Islam Rafi alongside monumental portrait busts completed during an outdoor studio residency, capturing scale, anatomical articulation, and ceramic sculptural practice.

2022

Exhibition Chronicles

Curatorial Exhibition Montage

Curated photographic record documenting national exhibition inaugurations, curatorial tours with dignitaries, gallery visitors, and ceremonial art salon honors.

2022

Plein-Air Display & Field Studies

Documentary Studio Photography

Outdoor exhibition documentation of recent plein-air landscape and boat studies displayed upon wooden tripods in daylight, showing the interplay between canvas and nature.

2022

Cry of the Collective

Plaster Relief Sculpture

A dramatic high-relief sculptural composition of intertwined human figures and expressive faces crying out in collective emotion, evoking social struggle and psychological resilience.

2022

Figure with Bird

Terracotta Ceramic Sculpture

A contemplative hand-built terracotta sculpture depicting a seated human figure in serene communion with a companion bird, embodying coexistence and timeless rural folklore.

2021

Studio Easel Study — Landscape in Progress

Watercolour & Acrylic on canvas

Contemporary studio study capturing layer development and atmospheric composition on easel.

2021

Anatomy of Being

Terracotta Totem Sculpture

A vertical terracotta vessel integrating sculpted human countenances with a visible ribcage framework, examining mortal fragility, corporeal memory, and sculptural architectural form.

2021

Scrap Metal Sentinels

Welded Scrap Metal Assemblage

A line of monumental outdoor figures constructed from reclaimed industrial steel and weathered metal remnants, exploring environmental recovery and the transformed machine age.

2020

Ascent of Man

Gilded Terracotta & Mixed Media

A dramatic upward-surging sculptural column finished in gold leaf, portraying multiple figures climbing and supporting one another in an enduring pursuit of collective elevation.

2020

Totem of Faces

Terracotta & Gold Leaf

A cylindrical terracotta column densely covered in dozens of expressive sculpted human portraits, with gold-accented faces in the center symbolizing enlightenment amid humanity.

2019

International Art Expo & Gallery Dialogues

Exhibition Documentation Collage

Archival photographic collage capturing exhibition rooms, curatorial discussions, public engagement, and group portraits at international art expositions and gallery showcases.
