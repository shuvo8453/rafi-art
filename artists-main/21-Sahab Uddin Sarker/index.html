Artist statement

Beyond the Physical World

Sahab Uddin Sarker is a Bangladeshi sculptor who works mainly in clay. His forms come from the land and water of the delta: a river carved through a block of stoneware, a char island with cracked silt, a country boat fired black with smoke, a crescent of ripening paddy.

He moves between the oldest ceramic traditions of Bengal and an open, experimental approach to form. Coiled terracotta vessels sit beside porcelain lotus petals and celadon leaves. The running stitch of nakshi kantha and the brick arches of village courtyards return as carved surface and architecture.

Across seven solo exhibitions and group shows in Bangladesh, South and Southeast Asia, Central Asia and Türkiye, his work has looked for what lies past the object itself: memory, place and the slow change of the land.
